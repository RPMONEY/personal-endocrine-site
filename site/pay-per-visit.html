Pricing and programs

Pay per visit

See Dr. Choi when you need to, without a membership.

You don’t have to join anything to see Dr. Choi. With pay per visit, you pay for each visit at the time of care, and you and Dr. Choi decide together how often follow-up makes sense.

It’s a good fit if you need a specialist evaluation or occasional follow-up for a condition that’s already well managed.

How it works

Initial consultation

Up to an hour with Dr. Choi to go through your history, symptoms, medications and goals.

Results call

A 10-minute call to walk through your lab results and what they mean for your care.

Follow-up as needed

Book follow-up visits in person or by telehealth when you and Dr. Choi agree they’re needed.

Good to know

A $75 deposit reserves your first appointment and is applied toward the cost of your visit. The deposit is non-refundable.

Personal Endocrine does not accept or bill insurance for visits. If you have commercial insurance, we can provide a superbill on request that you may submit for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Reimbursement depends on your individual plan. You can still use your insurance for labs, imaging and prescriptions, subject to your plan.
