Why we work outside insurance

Your care should be decided by you and your doctor. Not your insurance.

Insurance can influence how much time a doctor has with you, who you see and how often you’re seen. At Personal Endocrine, those decisions stay between you and Dr. Choi. You can still use your insurance for labs, imaging and prescriptions.

What is Personal Endocrine?

Personal Endocrine is a direct specialty care endocrinology practice led by Jinsun Choi, MD. Dr. Choi treats diabetes, obesity, thyroid and parathyroid conditions, osteoporosis, menopause, low testosterone and other endocrine concerns.

The practice is designed to give Dr. Choi more time with each patient and allow care decisions to be made without the constraints of insurance-based visits.

Do you accept insurance?

Personal Endocrine does not accept or bill insurance for visits. You pay the practice directly for your care.

If you have commercial insurance, we can provide a superbill on request that you may submit for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Reimbursement depends on your individual plan.

Do you accept Medicare?

No. Dr. Choi has opted out of Medicare. Visits are paid directly to Personal Endocrine and cannot be submitted to Medicare for reimbursement. Personal Endocrine also does not accept Medi-Cal.

Can I still use my insurance for labs, imaging and medications?

In many cases, yes. You may still use your insurance for labs, imaging and prescriptions, subject to your plan.

Coverage and reimbursement vary, so you are responsible for confirming the details of your individual insurance plan.

Is Personal Endocrine a concierge practice?

Personal Endocrine uses a direct specialty care model. You can pay for individual visits or choose a membership for ongoing care. Visits are paid directly to the practice rather than billed to insurance.

Do I have to become a member?

No. You can pay for individual services without joining a membership.

If you choose a membership, you may cancel with 30 days’ notice. If you later decide to re-enroll, a $300 re-enrollment fee applies.

Is there a deposit?

A $75 deposit reserves your first appointment and is applied toward the cost of your visit. The deposit is non-refundable.

Can I be seen by telehealth?
Yes. Follow-up visits are available in person or by telehealth.
Do I still need a primary care doctor?

Yes. Dr. Choi specializes in endocrinology and does not provide primary care.

We encourage patients to maintain an ongoing relationship with a primary care physician for healthcare needs outside of endocrinology.

What happens if Dr. Choi is away?

The Personal Endocrine team will continue to receive portal messages and emails. Dr. Choi can respond to messages, order labs and send prescriptions while she is away.

If she expects to be unavailable due to limited internet access, she will arrange coverage with another board-certified physician for urgent medical needs.
