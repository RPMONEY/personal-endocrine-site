What you pay us, and what insurance still covers

Personal Endocrine does not accept or bill insurance for visits. You can still use your insurance for the care that happens outside the exam room.

Paid directly to Personal Endocrine
  • Visits with Dr. Choi
  • Membership and program fees
  • Prior authorizations, appeals and forms, unless included in your plan
Still through your insurance
  • Labs
  • Imaging and other testing
  • Prescriptions

Coverage varies by plan, so please confirm the details with your insurer.

Good to know

New patient deposit
A $75 deposit reserves your first appointment and is applied toward the cost of your visit. The deposit is non-refundable.
Payment
Visits are self-pay and paid directly to Personal Endocrine at the time of your appointment.
Memberships
Membership is optional. You may cancel with 30 days’ notice. If you decide to rejoin later, a $300 re-enrollment fee applies.
Superbills
Have commercial insurance? We can provide a superbill for you to submit for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Reimbursement depends on your individual plan.
Medicare and Medi-Cal
Dr. Choi has opted out of Medicare, so visits cannot be submitted to Medicare for reimbursement. Personal Endocrine does not accept Medi-Cal.
Primary care
Personal Endocrine provides specialty endocrinology care, not primary care. Please continue seeing your primary care physician for healthcare needs outside endocrinology.
